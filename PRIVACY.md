# Privacy policy: Calm Quraan poster

Calm Quraan publishes short Quran recitation videos. This repository holds the small program that makes
the videos and schedules them on the Calm Quraan accounts, including the Calm Quraan Facebook Page through
a Meta app.

## What the app does

- It posts videos and captions to the Calm Quraan Facebook Page, which belongs to the person who runs this
  project. It is not offered to anyone else and nobody else can log in to it.
- It uses a Page access token, stored as an encrypted GitHub Actions secret, only to publish those posts.

## Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any person who sees, likes, comments
on or shares the videos. It does not read messages, comments or follower lists.

## Data deletion

There is no user data to delete, because the app stores none. To stop the app posting, the Page owner can
remove it at any time in Facebook: Settings > Business integrations (or Apps and websites) > remove the app.
That also invalidates its token.

## Contact

Questions: send a message to the Calm Quraan Facebook Page, or open an issue on this repository.
