# voice.md: Calm Quraan

Read by the ig-* skills in `.claude/skills/` (they look for `.claude/instagram/voice.md` in this repo).

## Who we are

- **Name:** Calm Quraan
- **Handle:** @calmquraan.daily (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook Page "Calmquraan.daily"); YouTube @calmquraandaily
- **What we do, in one sentence:** Short videos of well-known reciters reading popular Quran passages, with
  the Arabic and its English meaning on screen over real nature footage.
- **Who we are talking to:** Muslims (and the curious) scrolling at the end of a long day who want a calm
  minute with the Quran: English speakers first, a large share from South Asia and the Arab world.
- **What we sell:** Nothing. The goal is that people listen, reflect, comment a dhikr, and share it.

## What we sound like

- **Examples of our captions:**
  - "Think you've sinned too much to be forgiven?" then the verse (Az-Zumar 39:53) in Saheeh International.
  - "Trapped in darkness? Say what Yunus said." (Al-Anbiya 21:87-88)
  - "Going through something hard? Listen." (Ash-Sharh 94:1-8)
- **On camera:** faceless. No voiceover: the recitation is the audio. Text on screen: an opening line, the
  Arabic phrase in sync, the English meaning under it.
- **Words we use:** plain, warm, short. "Listen", "your heart", "your Lord", "Allah", the surah name.
- **Words we never use:** hype and marketing words (game-changer, insane, hack, viral, "stop scrolling"),
  fear-bait, "you won't believe", anything that treats the Quran as a trend.
- **Swearing:** never. **Emoji in captions:** none. **Face on camera:** never.
- **Pace:** not spoken by us; length follows the reciter (videos are 12 to 45 s, a weekly long one up to 3 min).

## Our positions

1. The Quran's own words are the hook; our line only points the viewer to them.
2. Calm beats loud: no music, no shouting text, real scenery.
3. Consistency over virality: a calm ayah every day.

## Off limits

- **Never invent or embellish meaning.** Translations are Saheeh International. Opening lines must stay true
  to the verses that are always on screen.
- **Virtues of surahs:** only authentic ones (e.g. Ayat al-Kursi before sleep, Al-Ikhlas a third of the Quran,
  the last two verses of Al-Baqarah at night, the first ten of Al-Kahf). Never weak narrations.
- **No rulings (fiqh), no politics, no sectarian topics, no comparing reciters negatively.**
- **Footage:** real nature only. No people, animals, buildings or religious symbols, and nothing AI-generated.
- **Do not rewrite a verse line to raise a hook score.** hookscore.py rewards numbers, money and stakes, which
  scripture lines are not built for.
